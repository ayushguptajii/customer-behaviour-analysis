# Customer Behaviour Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behaviour using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard focuses on customer demographics, purchasing patterns, product categories, subscription status, discounts, shipping preferences, and sales performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- DAX
- Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization
- Business Analysis

## 📈 Key KPIs

- **3.9K** Customers
- **3.75** Average Review Rating
- **$59.76** Average Purchase Amount
- **25** Products
- **4** Product Categories
- **50** Locations

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Customer Behaviour
The dashboard analyzes purchasing behaviour across different customer segments and age groups.

### Product Categories
Clothing records the highest purchase amount and customer volume among the categories shown in the dashboard.

### Subscription Status
- **73%** of customers are non-subscribers
- **27%** are subscribers

This indicates an opportunity to increase subscription adoption among existing customers.

### Discounts
The analysis compares discount usage across products and categories to identify opportunities for better discount optimization.

### Customer Segmentation
Customers are analyzed across different age groups to understand differences in revenue and sales behaviour.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Increase subscription adoption among existing customers.
2. Develop loyalty-focused offers for repeat customers.
3. Optimize product-level discounts based on customer behaviour.
4. Target high-value customer segments with personalized promotions.
5. Use customer and category insights to improve marketing decisions.
